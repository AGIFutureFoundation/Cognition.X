# State compliance — the fifty-state layer

> **Not legal advice.** Every figure below is flagged *verify* in the data: fees, forms and
> thresholds change yearly and are recorded as of **2025**. Confirm each with the named
> agency (or counsel) before filing. Nothing here asserts that Cognition.X is approved,

Generated by `tools/compliance_report.py` from
[`data/states/compliance.json`](../data/states/compliance.json) — the source the States app's

## What the layer covers

| Domain | What it is |
|---|---|
| Student data privacy | the law that governs a vendor or program touching student records, and the agreement a district will ask you to sign |
| Homeschool notice & assessment | what a family using the Homeschool role must file with the state or district |
| Nonprofit registration | foreign-nonprofit qualification, registered agent, annual report |
| Charitable solicitation | registration before asking residents for donations |
| Background checks | criminal-history checks for adults with unsupervised access to students |
| Mandated reporting | who must report suspected abuse and what training is required |
| Apprenticeship registration | which agency registers programs the trade tracks feed |
| CTE credential lists | the state's approved industry-recognized credential list |
| Digital accessibility | the standard a public entity's technology must meet |
| Sales-tax exemption | whether nonprofit purchases can be exempt |

## At a glance

- **38** states require charitable-solicitation registration before soliciting residents; **12** do not (or only with paid solicitors).
- **31** states have a SOPIPA-style statute binding ed-tech operators; the rest run on FERPA, COPPA and district policy.
- **28** states register apprenticeship programs through a State Apprenticeship Agency; **22** through the federal Office of Apprenticeship.
- Homeschool regulation: **11** no-notice · **15** low · **18** moderate · **6** high.
- Estimated one-time compliance cost per state: **$320–$1,400** under the stated assumptions (one program office, ten background checks; excludes staff time, counsel, insurance and devices).

## The national baseline

- FERPA (34 CFR Part 99) — student education records; the 'school official' exception governs any vendor a school lets touch records
- COPPA (16 CFR Part 312) — under-13 online data collection; a page that collects nothing on a server has nothing to send, but any hosted instance must comply
- PPRA — surveys and evaluations touching protected topics
- Section 504 / ADA Title II — accessibility of school technology (see accessibility)

**What the platform's architecture changes.** Cognition.X stores learner records only in the browser of the device in use; nothing leaves a page on its own and export is a deliberate act. That removes the server-side data flow most state operator laws regulate, but it does not remove the school's own duty: where a state requires a written data privacy agreement with any vendor or 'operator', sign one (the SDPC National Data Privacy Agreement with the state exhibit is the common instrument), and any hosted or synced deployment is a new review.

**Accessibility.** The 2024 ADA Title II web rule (28 CFR Part 35, Subpart H) requires state and local government web content and mobile apps — public schools included — to meet WCAG 2.1 AA: by April 24, 2026 for entities serving 50,000+ people, April 26, 2027 for smaller ones. Section 508 applies to federal funds recipients' procurement in many states' own policies. Every app carries skip links, lang, aria labels, live regions, keyboard operation and reduced-motion support (v0.33.0 first pass; v0.46.0 contrast verified ≥ 4.5:1). A full WCAG 2.2 AA audit is the open roadmap item — budget an external audit before a public-entity procurement.

**Background checks.** Every state fingerprints school employees; contractors and volunteers with unsupervised access to minors are covered by state law and district policy in varying ways. Assessors who witness checks in a school are, at minimum, subject to the district's volunteer/contractor policy. Typical cost per person: state + FBI fingerprint fee.

**Mandated reporting.** Every state has a mandated-reporter law; teachers and school staff are mandated reporters everywhere. Some states make every adult a mandated reporter ('universal'); several require training for school personnel. Training is usually free and online through the state child-welfare agency.

**Nonprofit registration.** A nonprofit organized in one state that runs programs, hires, or holds property in another usually must register there as a foreign nonprofit corporation (certificate of authority) with the Secretary of State, keep a registered agent in that state, and file an annual or biennial report. Merely licensing software or shipping a USB stick generally does not trigger it; running a cohort with staff on the ground usually does — ask counsel for the line in each state.

**Charitable solicitation.** About 40 states require a nonprofit that solicits contributions from their residents to register before asking (and to renew annually, often with the Form 990). Online donation pages reachable from a state can count as soliciting in it (the Charleston Principles). Fees are small; the labor is the calendar. Unified Registration Statement (URS) is accepted by most registration states; several require their own form.

**Apprenticeship.** Registered Apprenticeship programs are registered either with the federal Office of Apprenticeship (OA) or a DOL-recognized State Apprenticeship Agency (SAA). Registration is free. Cognition.X trade tracks are pre-apprenticeship curriculum: simulation ≠ certification, and a registered program's sponsor (the hall, the employer) holds the standards. (29 CFR Part 29 (standards) and Part 30 (EEO in apprenticeship))

**CTE credential lists.** Each state education agency keeps a list of approved industry-recognized credentials that count toward CTE accountability and graduation pathways. Cognition.X credentials are practice records, not industry certifications; the path onto a state list is an application by the credential's owner in the state's annual window, and it is not assumed here.

**Sales tax.** Nonprofit purchases are exempt from sales tax in some states on application, in others not at all; five states levy no state sales tax. Matters for devices and materials bought in-state.

## Maintaining the layer

Each state entry carries `sources` (the agencies to confirm with) and every fee carries `asOf` and `verify`.
A correction is a PATCH release: edit the JSON, run this script, and the tests will hold the shape.
The per-state checklist exports from the States app as `cxstate-compliance/1` JSON and prints.
