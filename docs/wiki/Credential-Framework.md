# Credential Framework

## One track, one credential

Each track names a credential (e.g. **Room & Building Explorer**,
**Machine Explorer**, **Data Steward**, **AI Navigator**). A learner earns
it by passing the track's transfer checks at their band; the credential
then deepens as they revisit the same themes at higher bands.

## The four levels

| Level | Bands | What it certifies |
|---|---|---|
| **Explorer** | K–2, 3–5 | Can recognise the terrain and perform the basic task with support |
| **Builder** | 6–8 | Can carry out the practice independently on familiar material |
| **Practitioner** | 9–10 | Can apply it to novel, real-world material and explain decisions |
| **Lead** | 11–12 | Can own the outcome, teach it, and handle the exception cases |

## Why transfer checks, not tests

A credential backed by a transfer check asserts something an employer,
landlord-support service or clinic can use: *this person restored a file
from their own backup; this person traced a claim to its source; this
person ran an account-recovery drill*. Verification is inspection of the
artifact, not recall of the content.
