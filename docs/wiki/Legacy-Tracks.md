# Legacy Tracks

**Legacy tracks** honour a named public figure's record by teaching the
principles of that record as coursework. The model comes from the
Education OS app's civic-leadership cornerstone, the **Willie L. Brown
Jr. Institute Track** (app iterations v28–v30): 333 courses built from
the Institute's public mission and the Willie L. Brown Jr. Fellowship
Program at San Francisco State — service as a craft, inclusion as a
design decision, hands-on placement inside real agencies, mentorship in
both directions, and pushing past ceilings.

## The localization template (app v29 "legacy tool")

The model localizes to any state edition by resolving eight slots:

| Slot | California original | Louisiana localization (v0.6.0) |
|---|---|---|
| Capital | Sacramento | Baton Rouge |
| Legislature | California Legislature | Louisiana Legislature |
| Local unit | County | **Parish** (64) |
| Standards framework | State standards | Louisiana student standards |
| Civic seal | State Seal of Civic Engagement | *verify* (marked per the v29 convention) |
| Anchor-industry hub | Mission Bay biotech | Ports, energy corridor, working coast |
| Placement ladder | City/county agencies | Parish offices, school boards, levee & special districts |
| Fellowship partner | SF State (public affairs) | State public-affairs school partner (to be agreed) |

## In the dataset

The first dataset-side legacy localization is the pack
**Civic Leadership Legacy : Louisiana**
([spec](https://github.com/AGIFutureFoundation/Cognition.X/blob/main/data/pack_specs/civic-leadership-legacy-louisiana.json),
250 blocks): the Louisiana ladder of public service, parish government,
the Legislature and the civil-law tradition, Louisiana's distinctive
elections, and the coastal civics of water and storm. Further state
localizations follow the same template and the standard pack shape
(5 tracks × 10 themes × 5 bands).

## Disclaimer (carried from the app, applies to every legacy track)

> This track is a proposal built from the Willie L. Brown Jr. Institute
> of Politics and Public Service's public mission and the Fellowship
> Program at San Francisco State University. It is **not affiliated
> with, endorsed by, or reviewed by** the Institute, SF State, or Mayor
> Brown. A named track would be offered under a partnership agreement
> and the Institute's review of every course.

Any new legacy track must carry the equivalent disclaimer in its spec
`notes`, name only public-record facts about the honoured figure, and be
marked as a proposal until the named organisation has agreed and
reviewed it.
