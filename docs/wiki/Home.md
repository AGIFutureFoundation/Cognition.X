# Cognition.X Wiki

**Flow Zone — education-based learning, as modular frameworks.**

Cognition.X decomposes learning into **blocks**: one capability, at one
grade band, proven by a **transfer check** on real material. Blocks roll
up into themes → tracks → credentials → packs, spanning K–12 math through
tenancy, reentry, global health field practice, and working with AI.
