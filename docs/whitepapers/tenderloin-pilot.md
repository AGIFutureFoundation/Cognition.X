## 1. What this is, in one paragraph

Cognition.X is an open-source, offline-first learning platform. Its Education OS carries a page titled *One building. One strand. Twelve weeks. Free.* On it are four mastery layers — 1,000 teaching blocks — written from four of THC's five published programmes, and one pilot small enough to be declined cheaply: one master-lease building, the Housing & Tenancy strand, twelve weekly ninety-minute sessions on paper, run by two THC staff, for eight to fifteen adult tenants who volunteer in the real sense. What THC would provide is a room, two staff and a decision about who is invited. What the platform provides is the materials, two hours of staff training and the printed packs, free, with no data leaving the room and nothing that can touch anybody's housing. This paper describes every layer, track, theme and session, the six refusals that bound the work, how the pilot would run and be measured, and how it would end.

## 3. From five programmes to four layers

| Their programme | What it became | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Permanent Housing | N · Housing & Tenancy | Tenancy, habitability, eviction and moving — the things a master-lease tenant is inside of. Taught as literacy, never as advice about a live case. |
| Money Management | O · Money, Benefits & Entitlements | Income, benefits, paying, saving, repair. Written for a budget with a payee, arrears and a benefits cliff in it, because that is the budget shape. |
| Recovery Services | P · Reentry & Recovery Pathways | The first thirty days, identity documents, work and the record, support, and the year after. Built around reporting requirements rather than ignoring them. |
| Community Organizing | Q · Neighbourhood, Safety & Civic Voice | The block, safety, code enforcement, the city, and two minutes at a hearing that decides something. |
| Legal Assistance | Not a layer, deliberately | THC has lawyers. This platform teaches what a notice is and what a deadline means; it does not advise, does not assess a case and does not stand between a tenant and their attorney. |

The design decision that matters most is the fifth row. THC has lawyers. This platform teaches what a notice is and what a deadline means; it does not advise, does not assess a case, and does not stand between a tenant and their attorney. Every housing block ends by naming the office rather than the answer.

## 4. How a mastery layer is built

Each layer is a pack of 250 blocks: five tracks, ten themes per track, five bands per theme (K–2, 3–5, 6–8, 9–10, 11–12). A block is the smallest unit of the platform: one sentence of what the learner does, and one **transfer check** — a thing a person does in front of another person, witnessed, that shows the learning transferred. The adult sessions in this pilot use the 11–12 band throughout; the younger bands exist because the same layers serve the platform's K–12 editions, and because a household often learns together.

Three rules of the platform apply to every block and are enforced by its build and tests, not by policy:

- **A credential is issued at exactly fifty witnessed checks**, signed offline by a Records Office key that never leaves the device, and names exactly what was witnessed. A track's credential names a capability (*Habitability Practitioner*), never a job title the credential cannot confer.
- **A simulation is practice.** Nothing rehearsed is ever a check or a credential.
- **Nothing leaves a page on its own.** The apps are single offline files with a Content-Security-Policy that blocks every network connection; an export is a person's act.

## 5. Six refusals, specific to this population

These are on the page in THC's terms, and they are the terms of the offer.

| Refusal | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **No legal advice, ever** | The Legal Assistance programme exists and is staffed by attorneys. Every housing block ends by naming the office rather than the answer; a learner asking about their own live matter is routed to a person, not to a module. |
| **No client record in this platform** | No name, no case, no housing status, no benefits detail, no recovery information. The file holds none and cannot transmit any. THC's systems remain THC's. |
| **No benefits determination** | A block explains what SSI is and what a recertification letter is. It never tells anybody what they are entitled to, because that is a determination and a wrong one costs somebody their income. |
| **No clinical or recovery advice** | The reentry layer covers documents, reporting, work and support structures. It says nothing about treatment, medication or a person's recovery. |
| **Nothing that can affect housing** | No completion, score, attendance or participation record may reach a tenancy decision. If a funder asks for participation data tied to housing, the answer is no and the pilot ends rather than bending. |
| **No claim of partnership** | Until THC signs something, this is an offer. |

**Why paper.** Tier 0 of the platform is the printed pack: nothing depends on a device, a connection, a phone or an account. A printed pack survives a room that has no wifi and a person who has no phone. The packs are generated from the same blocks as the app, so what is printed is what is published.

**Why two THC staff.** A community organiser and a case manager fit the shape. They hold every gate — who is invited, when a session runs, what is said about a live matter (nothing). The platform trains them once, in two hours, and then is not in the room.

**Why twelve weeks and one strand.** The platform's own critical-path page has listed "one site, one strand, one term" as an unclosed item since its earliest releases. This is the concrete version of it: small enough that a no costs THC fifteen minutes, and small enough that a yes costs a room and two staff-hours a week.

## 8. What is measured, what is not, and how it ends

- **Measured:** attendance, completion, and one question asked at week 12 and answered anonymously — *Did this change anything you did about your housing?* — published whatever it says.
- **Not measured:** anything about a person. No individual record leaves the room; the evaluation is a count and a set of anonymous answers.
- **How it ends:** a written report that THC owns. THC may publish it, sit on it, or use it to say the pilot failed. The platform may cite the outcome only with THC's permission and only as THC characterises it.

### City advisory work, and its limits

Twelve weeks of the civic-voice strand (layer Q) produces people who can read an agenda, find the department that decides, prepare two minutes and deliver it at a real hearing. That is a specific, teachable civic skill. The platform will not write the testimony, choose the position or represent anybody. A city department asking what a curriculum for tenants should contain is a reasonable question and the platform can answer it; a department asking for data about who attended is not, and the answer is the refusal in section 5. The order that matters: tenants first, the organisation second, the city third.

### How to start, if it is worth starting

| When | Step | What it involves |
|---|---|---|
| Today | Read this page and the refusals | Fifteen minutes. If anything here misdescribes how THC works, it is wrong and should be corrected before anything else happens. |
| This week | One conversation | Not a proposal. One organiser and one case manager, asked whether twelve sessions on tenancy literacy in one building would be useful or would be one more thing. |
| If yes | Print the twelve packs | They exist. Community Edition, free, no licence, no account, no procurement. Print them on a THC printer and nothing has been signed. |
| Week zero | Two hours of staff training | Delivered by this platform, in person or not, once. After it the staff run the sessions and this platform is not in the room. |
| Week one | Start | With eight people, in one building, on paper. |
| Week twelve | Write down what happened | Including if the answer is that it did not help. That report is THC’s, and a failed pilot published is worth more to everybody than a successful one that nobody can check. |

The most likely honest outcome is that THC reads this, decides its tenants have more pressing needs than a curriculum, and declines — a reasonable decision made by people who know their clients and do not know us. What is offered is deliberately small enough to be declined cheaply.

## 10. Appendix — the platform behind the page

- **Open source:** github.com/AGIFutureFoundation/Cognition.X. Curriculum content is CC BY 4.0; a Club, a clinic or a district may print, adapt and redistribute it.
- **Offline, single file:** each app is one HTML file that opens from a USB stick; no account, no server, no telemetry, no third-party script. A strict Content-Security-Policy is enforced by the browser.
- **Records:** completion records are signed offline (ECDSA P-256) and verifiable without the platform; Open Badges 3.0 / W3C Verifiable Credential envelopes are available. In this pilot no records are kept at all.
- **Accessibility:** audited at WCAG 2.2 AA (axe-core over 47 views, zero WCAG-tagged violations, keyboard sweep clean); printed packs are the accessible path where no device is.
- **Data quality and honesty:** every generated document and dashboard is rebuilt in CI and fails on drift; the platform's own content debt is published on its data-quality page rather than hidden.
