    From blueprint to running system

    How a state adopts

    The Louisiana platform is the working reference: a two-wave adoption plan computed from the state's own fact base, regional Trade Halls, launch curriculum, readiness boards, budgets, a learner ledger with signed credentials, and the flow automations over all of it.

    1. Seed the fact base — the state's subdivisions (counties, parishes, boroughs), hubs, industries and narrative worlds, on the Louisiana pattern.
    2. Adopt the spine — K–12, life skills, emergency response, the Parish Launch & Scale rollout pack and universal access, plus this blueprint localized.
    3. Plan two waves — the largest-reach cohort first (Louisiana: 33 of 64 parishes, ~88% of population in year one), statewide in year two.
    4. Stand up the halls — regional Trade Halls with the Network OS automations keeping learners and teachers in flow.
    5. Run the ledger — browser-local learner records, standing dashboards for every seat, and signed portable credentials from each hall's Records Office.

    Working reference implementation: Cognition.X Louisiana · curriculum engine: Flow Hub · trades layer: Trades Network.
